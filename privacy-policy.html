Your Data, Your Rights

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Aurelia ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects the personal data you share with us through our website (www.yourdomain.com) or when you contact our restaurant.

2. Personal Data Processed

If you fill out the contact form or place an order through our website, the following personal data of yours is processed:

  • Identity data: first and last name
  • Contact data: e-mail address, phone number (if provided)
  • Order data: items ordered and order timestamp
  • Other: the content of the message you send us

3. How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is processed solely for the purposes of responding to your requests and messages, managing customer relations, processing orders and reservation requests, and maintaining communication with you. Your data is not used for any purpose other than those specified.

4. Legal Basis

We process your personal data based on the consent you give when submitting our contact form or placing an order, and where necessary to fulfill a request you've made or pursue legitimate business interests in a way that does not override your rights.

5. How We Share Your Data

Our website does not transmit or store the information you enter in the contact or reservation forms itself. When you submit a form, your browser opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message for you to review and send; from that point on, the message is handled directly by WhatsApp (operated by Meta) as a conversation between you and our business, under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

Your personal data is retained for as long as required by the purpose of processing, subject to the statutory limitation periods set out in applicable legislation. It is deleted, destroyed, or anonymized within a reasonable period after your request has been resolved.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR, CCPA, or other local regulations) may give you the following rights:

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed,
  • Request access to the data we hold about you,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • Know which third parties your data has been shared with,
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data,
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to applicable legal requirements,
  • Object to automated decision-making that affects you,
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

8. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise the rights listed above, you may submit your request by e-mail to you@example.com or in writing to our restaurant address. Your request will be concluded free of charge as soon as possible, and no later than 30 days, depending on its nature.

9. Cookies

Our website uses browser storage for technical purposes such as remembering the contents of your cart (localStorage). This information is kept on your device and does not serve any purpose of tracking your identity. Our website does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

10. General Information

As Aurelia, we respect the privacy of all guests who visit our website and contact us. Accordingly:

  • Your personal data is processed only for explicitly stated purposes and to the extent necessary,
  • Your data is protected with reasonable technical and administrative measures,
  • Your data is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes without your consent,
  • This policy may be updated in line with changes in legislation or our business processes; the current version is always published on this page.

11. Contact

For questions about our privacy policy or the processing of your personal data, you can reach us through our contact page or directly at you@example.com.
