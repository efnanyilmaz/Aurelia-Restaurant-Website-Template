This folder is intentionally empty of video files (hero-video.mp4, coffee.mp4,
espresso.mp4 aren't included) — the originals were real footage from an actual
restaurant, used only for building/demoing this template, and aren't licensed
for redistribution.

The site works perfectly well without video: every <video> tag has a poster
image already set, so visitors just see a still photo in that spot until you
either add your own clip or remove the <video> tag.

To add your own video, drop it into this folder using the exact filename below:

  - coffee.mp4     Used on the homepage, in the "Our Story" section
                    (recommended: under 8MB, muted/autoplay-friendly, no audio needed)
  - espresso.mp4   Used on the About page, in the "Our Kitchen" section
                    (same recommendations as above)

hero-video.mp4 is not currently used by any page — ignore it unless you plan
to add a full-bleed hero video yourself.

Don't want video at all? Nothing to do — leave this folder empty and the
poster images will keep showing.
